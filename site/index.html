    How it works

    Every number comes from a benchmark's own published leaderboard. Each benchmark is rescaled so the leader scores 100 and the middle of the ranked models 50, benchmarks are averaged into skills, and skills are combined by weight. A skill nobody has tested a model on yet is estimated and marked with a *. Only models released in the last 120 days, with at least one coding result and at least half their score measured, are ranked.

    Scores within a point or two of each other are a tie. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. How it works goes through every step.

    What is this?

    WizardIndex ranks AI models on the actual job: shipping code, having taste, reasoning, and not falling apart the moment something is unfamiliar.

    No vendor-reported numbers. No launch-day hype. Every result links back to where it was published, so you can check our work instead of trusting it.
